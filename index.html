Sterile Console

A touchless pointing study for the operating room — comparing direct-mouse control against a simulated gesture-tracked, no-touch control mode.

Scenario. A surgeon needs to review imaging (CT slices, angiography) mid-procedure without breaking sterility. Two control modes are compared:

Baseline — direct mouse control, as if a non-scrubbed assistant were operating the console.
Sterile / Touchless — the same pointing task, but the on-screen cursor now behaves like an optically-tracked hand gesture: it lags behind your real movement and carries a small unsupported-arm tremor.

You will alternately click two glowing targets as they appear, first under Baseline, then under Sterile. 9 distance/width combinations × 6 repetitions are tested per mode (108 taps total). Try to be as fast and accurate as you can — errors are recorded.
